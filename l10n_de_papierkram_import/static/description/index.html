Move from Papierkram to Odoo

Contacts, invoices, vouchers, payments and fixed assets straight from your Papierkram exports, without typing anything again

What is taken over

  • Contacts with their customer and supplier numbers
  • Customer invoices, cancellations and credit notes
  • Expense and income vouchers, booked on the SKR03 or SKR04 accounts of their categories
  • Payments as cash entries with their own payment date
  • Fixed assets with their depreciation
  • The original PDF documents, attached to their entries
  • The complete history, or only the open items: unpaid invoices and vouchers with their partial payments
  • Runs again without duplicates: documents already imported are skipped
  • Small business (§ 19 UStG) or regular VAT
  • Check first: the log lists every issue before anything is booked
  • Every entry keeps its Papierkram number

How it works

  1. In Papierkram, create one export Vollständig (CSV, PDF) for every year under Einstellungen → Export und Import. Recommended: the fixed assets (Ausgaben → Anlagegüter, CSV) and your category list with its SKR03 accounts.
  2. In Odoo, go to Invoicing → Configuration → Papierkram Import, create an import and upload the files.
  3. Choose the scope and the settings.
  4. Click Check and read the log, then click Import.

Your invoices, ready in Odoo

Works together with

  • Germany EÜR (l10n_de_euer), the cash-basis profit statement: the module Papierkram EÜR Assets takes the fixed assets into its asset register.
  • Timesheets (sale_timesheet): the module Papierkram Timesheets imports products, time entries and the unbilled work.

Both are installed automatically as soon as their requirements are.

Good to know

  • Requires the German chart of accounts SKR03 or SKR04 (l10n_de).
  • Do not set a lock date before importing: Odoo would move entries of a locked period and change payment dates.
  • Invoices are taken over with their totals, as Papierkram's CSV has no line items; the original PDF is attached.
  • Bank transactions are not imported.
  • Regular VAT with several tax rates in one voucher is not covered yet.
